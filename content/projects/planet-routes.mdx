## The problem

Visit every planet on the map, starting and ending at fixed points, with a
route that is short without being exhaustively optimal.

## Approach

**Parse, then plan.** Mission bounds, planet coordinates and planet names come
from separate input files and are loaded into `Planet` and `Mission` structs.
Out-of-bounds planets are filtered before planning starts.

**Nearest neighbour.** From the current position, the planner picks the
closest unvisited planet by Euclidean distance, moves there, marks it visited,
and repeats. It's a greedy heuristic rather than an optimal tour, but it's fast
and good enough in practice.

**Readable output.** The program prints the map as a character grid, then the
journey step by step, from "Start at" to "End at".
